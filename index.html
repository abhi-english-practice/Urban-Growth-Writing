IELTS Writing Task 2 · Effects & Solutions · Model essay & practice

Model Essay

Population in cities is growing rapidly. What are the effects of this? What solutions can you give to maintain a good quality of life in urban areas?

Writing Architecture

INTRODUCTION

Paraphrase + thesis

Rapid urban population growth → adverse effects → immediate and strategic solutions.

BODY 1

Effects

Housing shortages → rising property prices → informal settlements. Public-service pressure → congestion and overloaded hospitals.

BODY 2

Solutions

Urban planning and satellite towns → decentralization. Infrastructure investment → transport, healthcare, green spaces.

CONCLUSION

Restate the argument

Housing and service challenges can be addressed through planning and infrastructure investment.

Writing7 · Language moves

Cause → when the demand ... exceeds the available supply
Consequence → Consequently, property prices escalate
Solution → One effective approach is to implement ...
Result → As a result, this population dispersal reduces ...
Concession → although rapid urban population growth poses ...
